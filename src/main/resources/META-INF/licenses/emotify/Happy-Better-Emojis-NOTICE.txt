Emotify includes the original faces.png and animals.png atlases without visual modification. The built-in catalog indexes all 162 available 8x8 icons: 130 face icons and 32 animal icons.

Changes made by Emotify:

- the atlases were moved from the minecraft namespace to the emotify namespace;
- stable Emotify IDs, English and Russian translations, categories, Unicode metadata, and source slots were assigned;
- all regions are selected through Emotify's manifest and UV mapping;
- the visual artwork itself was not modified.
